María Fernández Barrera is a PhD candidate at the Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science, Universitat de Barcelona, and a member of the AIBA research group, supervised by Petia Radeva, Carles Maria Falcon Falcon and Ahmad AlMughrabi. Her research focuses on artificial intelligence and computer vision applied to biology and medicine. Before the PhD she spent nearly 20 years as an R&D and engineering leader, turning Data & AI research into production systems for regulated industries. She holds an MSc in Biomedical Data Science from the Universitat Rovira i Virgili, an MSc in Advanced and Applied Artificial Intelligence from the Universitat de València, and a First Class BSc (Hons) in Computing from Edinburgh Napier University.
