My work explores how AI methods can learn meaningful representations from structured data, combining machine learning research with practical applications. ## Research ### NutritionBERT: A Physics-Informed Masked Transformer for Nutritional Representation Learning and Profile Prediction from Food Composition Data My research investigates transformer-based representation learning for food composition data, integrating nutritional structure and domain-informed constraints to support nutritional profile prediction. The work uses the BLS food composition dataset, with 7,140 food items and 136 nutritional features. ## Research interests - Artificial intelligence and machine learning - Transformer architectures and representation learning - Data-centric AI and structured data - Responsible and applied AI ## Contact Please feel free to get in touch to discuss research, collaboration, and related work.
